About the Mall

Kisii's Premier Commercial Destination

KampDavid Mall stands as a bold architectural statement in the heart of Kisii — a modern multi-storey commercial complex featuring a striking geometric façade of bronze and glass that defines the city skyline.

Designed to attract high foot traffic, the mall offers flexible retail, office, and service spaces across multiple levels. With its distinctive cylindrical glass tower and patterned mosaic cladding, KampDavid Mall is built for businesses that demand presence and excellence.
